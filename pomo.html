Pomo-Pal

Background-Desktop game designed to help you study. Collect all kinds of pomo-pals the more you study! As you take breaks, play minigames, feed them, and watch them sleep (cutely)!

Pomo-Pals!

Main Theme during the daytime, plays as you feed & play with your pomo-pal.
I wanted this to be uplifting and relaxed, to match gameplay!

Kawaii!!!

Title Theme of the game, I wanted a very energizing start,
and for it to develop as it played to keep player attention!

Zzz...

As you can guess, this was for the night, when your pomo-pal was asleep.
I gave this a very soft, lush piano that subtley developed during
the song to a lower tone. This creates a calming sensation!
