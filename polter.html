Polter-Glitch

First-Person action-stealth game with horror elements.
You play as a self-aware virus, trying to hack your way out of the clutches of the TV-head workers.

Into the Screen

The Title Track, which I wanted to convey themes of glitchiness,
as well as action and stealth.

~Blue Screen

Builds up the moment right before the boss-fight,
as the player approaches they realise something is off.

Of Death~

The Boss Fight, a stressful and climactic theme
with heavy drums and sirens blasting!

Some SFX

Some simple SFX like walking & sneaking, and a static noise
that would play whenever the enemy (a TV head) got close.
