Last Stop

Narrative-Puzzle game, where you play as a last-minute detective trying to solve a messy murder case. You arrive to a gas station at 3am, and work with what you can find to interrogate and lock up the culprit.

Dialogue System

The dialogue is inspired by Animal Crossing.
I recorded myself making vowel noises,
and created different presets for each character
with various pitches and tempos.

Stereo Ride

Title Track, which I wanted to be laid-back and chill,
before the mystery of the game takes off.
(Sirens are also incorporated for thematic reasons!)

Hello & Welcome

As the player enters the murder scene, this track lures them in,
slowly building as they're sucked further into the narrative.
